# Humanoid animation rules

## Source, rig and skin
- Production humanoids are `unityRigType: Humanoid` with Unity-style scaffold bones and separate parts (staff, bow, quiver, weapons) socketed on `Socket_Hand_L/R`. The production FBX and textures are immutable inputs.
- Meshy rigs the body only. The resulting Mixamo-template skeleton (`mixamorig:*`) is kept; a stationary `Root` bone is added above `mixamorig:Hips` so gates and Unity get a navigation root.
- The provider skin is a welded copy of the body (fewer vertices). Its weights are transferred by nearest surface onto the exact production body **with both meshes in rest pose** (unbind every action and set the armature to REST first, or the mapping is garbage). Record the max surface distance.
- Rigid parts are skinned to one hand bone with full weight (pivot-safe across FBX); they must sit in the hand in the rest pose, which holds when the rig input came from the same A-pose model.

## Motions
- Presets are chosen per role: idle/breathing; walk or run whose implied speed can be bound to the gameplay move speed; weapon-appropriate basic attack (staff casters use a Mage Spell Cast variant, not a melee swing); a distinct cast/summon for casters; hit reaction; dying that ends on the ground.
- Presets are 24 fps; the packager resamples to 30 fps by evaluating real poses, strips horizontal root travel (knockback, death slide) so clips are in place, and extracts foot contacts from the walk so planted-sole drift can be measured.
- Meshy in-place walks are treadmills whose implied speed is far below hero move speeds (Malachar: 1.2 u/s vs 7.1 u/s). Either take a running preset or bind the Locomotion rate at runtime; never fake it with a global animator speed.
- Known provider defects to polish before install: feet slip on the walk, dead pose sinking through the floor, idle seams that do not loop. Fix in the package, never by loosening gates.
- Meshy retargets rotation only: on legs with other proportions the human leg angles push the soles under the floor (ogre: 20 cm) and each foot follows its own path, so no hip shift alone can plant both feet. The packager passes, in order: `--posture-relax Idle=k Locomotion=k` (spine/neck/head blended toward rest so a hunched preset keeps the head visible), `--ground-clamp` (per-frame hip lift so the lowest body vertex rests on the floor; rigid parts excluded; smoothed with an element-wise max so nothing sinks), `--plant-feet` (horizontal hip shift so the mean planted-foot motion is one uniform treadmill; records the resulting nominal speed), `--lock-feet` (leg IK pins each planted ankle to that treadmill at the stance's lowest height with the sole and toe orientation of that frame, faded over 4 swing frames, then a second clamp). Every pass writes its numbers into the receipt.
- Brute proportions need three more passes: `--leg-splay <rad>` swings each upper leg outward about the forward axis (sign fixed from the rest pose, soles counter-rotated flat) so a wide-stanced body does not walk with its thighs glued together; the foot lock then lowers the pelvis by a clip-wide constant when the splayed legs cannot reach (a per-frame dip lurches at every stance change) and seats the sole, not the ankle, on the floor. `--hand-guard` runs after a hands-excluded ground clamp: a small hand penetration bends the elbow, a large one (an arm lying through the floor) swings the whole arm about the shoulder within its own vertical plane, biased outward from the hips, so it lies on the ground; a final full clamp catches the rest. Without it the clamp lifts the whole body to keep long arms out of the floor (the ogre hopped 40 cm at its slam).
- Rigid parts are seated with `--grip Part=bone,fraction,up|down`: the fist's principal axes give finger direction, palm normal and knuckle line; the shaft runs along the knuckle line with the point at `fraction` from the handle end (the end the modeler left nearer the fist) at the fist centre. Shields stay rigid on the forearm without a grip. Rigid-skinning the part where the modeler left it makes the shaft continue the forearm.
- Provider clips rarely loop or settle: `--loop-blend 6` eases each looping clip's tail into its first pose; `--death-hold <s>` appends a held final pose (Fall Down only reaches the ground in its last fifth, so it needs 1.5 s). Robed bodies weight the hem to the foot bones: set `soleWeightThreshold` (0.85) in the subject's calibration so the review and the foot lock measure the sole, not the hem.
- Contacts come from the ankle height (≤3% h above its minimum) refined by treadmill velocity when that keeps at least half the frames; the review measures planted-sole drift against the receipt's nominal speed, so `--plant-feet` must run before `--lock-feet`.
- Gate scope: Death is judged by its own core-drop, rotation and settle rules and is exempt from the region-drop detachment check (a flat corpse drops every region by design). Idle and Locomotion keep the 10% posture-drop rule; relax the posture rather than the rule.
- Rigid parts are not part of the floor-penetration gate; check them in the frames (a `down` grip put the ogre club 15 cm under the floor while the gates passed).

## Gates and evidence
- Same `gates.py` thresholds as creatures; evidence under `docs/visual-qa/animation-quality/plan-011/<subject>/v<N>/` (review.json, preview reels, README, unity-integration.json).
- Death for humanoids must drop the core ≥15% body height and hold; attack/cast/hit must return to a neutral end pose.
